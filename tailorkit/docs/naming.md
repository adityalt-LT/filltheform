# Product name

**Current working name:** Tailor & Apply. It describes the product, but it is long, hard to turn into a domain, and unlikely to be ownable (generic words are hard to protect and hard to rank for).

**Important:** I could not check domain availability, trademarks or existing products from here. Everything below is a shortlist to *test*, not a cleared name. Do the checks in section 4 before you spend money or announce anything.

## 1. What the name has to do

- **Say "your resume, fitted to the job" without saying "AI resume builder"**, which is a crowded search term.
- **Be short** (2 syllables is ideal), easy to say on a call, easy to spell from hearing it once.
- **Work in India first** and still travel: easy for Hindi and English speakers.
- **Be distinctive enough to rank for**: a word that is also an everyday noun (for example "tailor") is nearly impossible to own in search.
- **Leave room to grow** beyond resumes (job tracking, interview prep, the radar).

## 2. Shortlist

Scores are my judgement from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) on the criteria I can assess without looking anything up. Domain and trademark are deliberately unscored: unchecked.

| Name | Idea | Memorable | Says "fit/tailoring" | India resonance | Distinct for search | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Darzi** | Hindi/Urdu for "tailor" | 5 | 5 | 5 | 3 | Common word and surname; likely used by tailoring businesses; needs a qualifier in search ("Darzi resume") |
| **Naap** | Hindi for "measurement": a tailor takes your *naap* | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | Meaning is lost on non-Hindi speakers |
| **Dhaaga** | Hindi for "thread": the thread that connects you to the job | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | Spelling variants (dhaga/dhaaga) |
| **Tailorly** | Direct and friendly | 3 | 5 | 2 | 2 | Generic; probably crowded; hard to own |
| **Hemline** | Tailoring term; the finishing edge | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 | Fashion associations |
| **Shortlistd** | The outcome you want | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | Deliberate misspelling hurts word of mouth |
| **Fitted** | Fit to the job + tailoring | 4 | 5 | 3 | 1 | Very common word; surely taken |
| **Offerfit** | Fit and offers | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Reads as a recruiter tool |

## 3. Recommendation

1. **Darzi** as the first choice, with the line *"Your resume, tailored."* It carries the whole idea in one familiar word, it sounds like a brand and not a feature, and it is instantly warm to an Indian audience. Plan for the search problem from day one: always pair it with context ("Darzi: resume tailoring"), and never rely on the bare word for ranking. Likely domain forms: `darzi.ai`, `darzi.app`, `getdarzi.com`, `darzi.in` (the exact `.com` is probably gone).
2. **Naap** if Darzi is unavailable or too generic: shorter, more ownable in search, same metaphor.
3. **Tailorly** only as a fallback if you need a name that works with no explanation at all.

Keep **Tailor & Apply** as the working name until a name clears the checks. It is one line to change (section 5).

## 4. Checks before you commit (about an hour, mostly free)

1. **Domains:** `.com`, `.in`, `.ai`, `.app` for the exact name and a `get`/`try` variant. Check price too: some are held for resale.
2. **Trademarks:**
   - India: the Trade Marks Registry public search, classes **9** (software), **35** (business services), **41** (career and training), **42** (SaaS). Also look at class 25/40 for tailoring, to understand the overlap.
   - Global: WIPO Global Brand Database and the US and UK registers, in case you expand.
   - A lawyer's quick clearance opinion is cheap compared with a rebrand.
3. **Existing products:** search the name plus "resume", "jobs" and "app"; the Chrome Web Store; the Play and App stores; LinkedIn company pages.
4. **Handles:** Instagram, X, LinkedIn page, YouTube, GitHub, Product Hunt.
5. **Say it out loud:** to five people who have never seen it; ask them to write it down. Count how many spell it correctly.
6. **Pronunciation and meaning** in the languages your users speak (no unfortunate meanings).

Pass: the name is free in the classes that matter, a usable domain exists, and four of five people spell it right.

## 5. How to rename

1. Edit `web/brand.js` (`name`, `mark`, `domain`, emails, legal details).
2. Run `npm run brand`. It updates the extension manifest and popup text and the dashboard page title.
3. Rebuild the website and demo (`npm run site:build`, `npm run demo:build`); both read the same file.
4. Update the Chrome Web Store listing and the legal pages (they use the same brand file).

## 6. Positioning line to pair with any name

> Apply with a resume that fits every job. Same layout, better match, and an interview brief for each role.

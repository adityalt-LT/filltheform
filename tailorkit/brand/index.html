Rolemate, in motion.

Two cards overlap: the role and you. Where they meet is the match. Two animated versions of the logo follow.

Version 1

Assemble and spell

The two cards slide together, the overlap pops, the check draws itself, then the name spells out one letter at a time. Just under 3 seconds.

What is included

  • Animated SVG: version 1 and 2, light and dark. They play once and hold the final frame. In browsers set to reduce motion they show the final frame only.
  • Static SVG: mark, mark on dark, lockup, lockup on dark, favicon (no check, for tab sizes).
  • PNG icons: 16, 32, 48, 128, 180 and 512 px.
  • The wordmark is converted to outlines, so no font is needed. The earlier Check-R option is kept in a separate folder.
